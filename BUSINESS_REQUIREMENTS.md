### **Business Requirements: New E-commerce Theme for Bagisto**

**1. Project Overview & Vision**

We are developing a new, modern, and highly intuitive theme for our Bagisto e-commerce platform. The primary goal is to create a clean, user-friendly, and visually appealing shopping experience that drives conversions and enhances brand loyalty.

The theme must be designed for a **multi-tenant architecture**. This means the core design should be consistent and robust, while also allowing for easy customization (e.g., logo, primary colors, banner images) for different business tenants.

**2. Core Business Goals**

*   **Increase Conversion Rate:** The design should guide users seamlessly from product discovery to checkout completion.
*   **Enhance User Engagement:** Create an intuitive and enjoyable browsing experience that encourages users to explore products.
*   **Mobile-First Experience:** The theme must be fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices, as a majority of users will shop on their phones.
*   **Strengthen Brand Identity:** The visual design should be modern, trustworthy, and reflect our brand's values.

**3. Target Audience**

*   **Primary Persona:** Tech-savvy millennials and Gen Z shoppers (ages 18-40) who are comfortable shopping online. They value clean aesthetics, fast-loading pages, and a hassle-free checkout process. They shop on both mobile and desktop devices.

**4. Branding & Visual Identity (To Be Provided by You)**

This is the most critical section for the designer. Please provide them with the following:

*   **Brand Keywords:** (e.g., "Modern," "Minimalist," "Vibrant," "Trustworthy," "Elegant")
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary Color:** (e.g., The main brand color for buttons, links, and highlights)
    *   **Secondary Color:** (e.g., For accents, promotions)
    *   **Neutral Colors:** (e.g., Backgrounds, text, borders - like shades of grey, white)
*   **Typography:**
    *   **Heading Font:** (e.g., Poppins, Montserrat)
    *   **Body Font:** (e.g., Lato, Open Sans)
*   **Logo:** Provide high-resolution logo files in various formats (e.g., SVG, PNG).

**5. Required Pages & Key Features**

The designer must provide high-fidelity mockups for the following pages and components:

*   **A. Homepage:**
    *   **Hero Section:** A large, eye-catching banner or carousel for promotions.
    *   **Featured Categories:** Visually appealing cards to direct users to main product categories.
    *   **New Arrivals/Featured Products:** A product grid or slider to showcase key items.
    *   **Promotional Banners:** Sections for marketing messages or sales.
    *   **Newsletter Signup:** A simple form to capture user emails.
    *   **Footer:** With links to company info, customer service, and social media.

*   **B. Product Listing Page (Category Page):**
    *   **Grid & List View:** Allow users to toggle between views.
    *   **Advanced Filtering:** A clear, easy-to-use sidebar for filtering by price, brand, size, color, and other attributes.
    *   **Sorting Options:** Dropdown for sorting by "Price: Low to High," "Newest," etc.
    *   **Product Card:** Must clearly display product image, name, price, and an "Add to Cart" or "Quick View" button.

*   **C. Product Detail Page:**
    *   **Image Gallery:** Main product image with thumbnails for alternate views and zoom functionality.
    *   **Product Information:** Name, price, SKU, and a short description.
    *   **Product Options:** Clean selectors for variants like size and color.
    *   **Quantity Selector.**
    *   **"Add to Cart" Button:** Prominent and clear.
    *   **Detailed Description:** Full product details, specifications.
    *   **Customer Reviews & Ratings.**
    *   **Related Products/You May Also Like:** A slider or grid of recommended items.

*   **D. Shopping Cart & Checkout:**
    *   **Cart Page/Drawer:** A clear summary of items, quantities, and subtotal. Easy to update or remove items.
    *   **Checkout Flow:** A simple, multi-step process:
        1.  **Information:** Guest checkout option, login, and shipping address form.
        2.  **Shipping:** Shipping method selection.
        3.  **Payment:** Payment method selection (credit card, PayPal, etc.).
        4.  **Review:** Final order summary before placing the order.
    *   **Order Confirmation Page:** A clear "Thank You" message with order details.

*   **E. User Account Pages:**
    *   **Dashboard:** Overview of recent orders and account info.
    *   **Order History:** A list of past orders with status.
    *   **Address Book:** Manage saved shipping and billing addresses.
    *   **Profile Settings:** Update name, email, and password.
    *   **Wishlist:** A page to view saved items.

*   **F. Static Pages:**
    *   About Us
    *   Contact Us (with a contact form)
    *   FAQ
    *   Terms & Conditions

**6. Technical & Functional Requirements for the Designer**

*   **Responsive Design:** All designs must be mobile-first and adapt flawlessly to tablet and desktop screen sizes.
*   **Accessibility:** Designs should follow WCAG 2.1 AA guidelines to be usable by people with disabilities.
*   **Performance:** Visual elements (like images and animations) should be optimized for fast page load times. Avoid overly complex or heavy graphics.
*   **Interactivity:** Provide designs for interactive states like hover effects on buttons, active form fields, and loading indicators.

**7. Deliverables**

*   High-fidelity mockups for all pages and components listed above (for mobile and desktop).
*   A comprehensive Style Guide or Design System (defining colors, typography, spacing, button styles, etc.).
*   All necessary assets, such as icons and illustrations, in SVG format.
*   Prototypes demonstrating the primary user flows (e.g., discovery to checkout).
*   All source files, preferably in **Figma**.
